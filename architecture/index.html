From a plot to a tower: how we plan, draw, engineer and build it

01 / 06 · Developer · architects

It starts with your plot.

We look at the land, the road and the rules, then work out where the tower, podium, parking and gardens will go.

  • Setbacks and approvals checked first
  • Parking and fire access planned

02 / 06 · 2D designers

Then each floor, flat by flat.

We draw a typical floor properly: four flats around a lift and stair core, with kitchens, bedrooms and balconies placed for light and air.

  • Flats sized for real families
  • Light and ventilation checked

03 / 06 · Architects

Then the outside of the building.

The elevation settles the height, the number of floors, the balconies and the materials before anyone builds a model.

  • Heights fixed early
  • Facade chosen with you

04 / 06 · 3D designers

Then you see it in 3D.

We sketch the tower in lines and then render it floor by floor, so you see the whole building before it exists.

  • Realistic 3D views and films
  • Changes made before work starts

05 / 06 · Structural designers

Then we make sure it stands.

Footings, columns, a shear core and slabs, designed floor by floor and checked before the site opens.

  • Columns and slabs for every floor
  • Drawings checked before site work

06 / 06 · Developer · site team

Then we build it.

Our own team raises it floor by floor, until the drawing becomes the building you pictured.

  • Quality checks at every stage
  • Progress reports for you
